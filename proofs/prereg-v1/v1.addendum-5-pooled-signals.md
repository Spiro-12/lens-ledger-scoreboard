# Pre-registration v1, addendum 5: the pooled "many weak clues" test

**Status:** stamped with OpenTimestamps and published as a fingerprint before any secret it counts exists. Approved by Spiro on 2026-11-17 (`decisions/2026-11-17-pooled-signals.md`). It adds two pre-registered **secondary results**. Under §9 these are never called findings; only P1–P3 are. It changes no frozen parameter: P1–P3, the single-lens promotion bar (§7.4) and the accounts of §3 are untouched. Where a reading has to be chosen, the more conservative one applies.

**Why.** Under §7.4, each lens must clear the promotion bar alone. A lens with a real but weak edge may never clear it, even when ten such lenses together would. The best quant firms reportedly pool many weak signals in one model (`research/quant_masters.md`, E1). Here the pool is tested as **one** hypothesis, so the multiple-testing penalty is paid once.

## H-POOL-A: does the desk as a whole beat random? (runs from now)

1. **Unit.**
   - A unit is the latest scored row of a secret whose `counts_for_ladder` is true under §7.3 and addendum 4, as written by the judge.
   - The secret must be sealed after the Bitcoin-attested block time of this addendum's OpenTimestamps proof. Secrets sealed before that attestation is confirmed don't count.
   - Watch-only and not-filled secrets are excluded, and their counts are reported.
2. **Lift.** The unit's `lift` field as the judge computes it: dollars per secret, the secret's profit after fees minus its baseline, summed over its filled legs.
3. **Lenses.**
   - Every lens enters automatically from its first unit. No lens can be left out, before or after results.
   - A retired lens's past units stay in the pool.
4. **Clusters.**
   - Each unit's cluster date is the UTC date of the latest scheduled close among its markets, as recorded at the seal.
   - An event-day is a distinct (event, cluster date) pair.
5. **Statistic and bound.**
   - Let X_d be the mean lift of the units on cluster date d.
   - Date d enters at the earlier of two times: when every secret with that cluster date has a scored row with no pending proof, or d + 30 days. Units that arrive later are left out and counted. Dates enter in date order. Once entered, X_d never changes.
   - The statistic is the mean of the X_d entered so far, with t the number of dates entered.
   - The bound comes from the two-sided normal-mixture asymptotic confidence sequence ("Time-uniform central limit theory and asymptotic confidence sequences"). The formula below governs:
     - ρ = √((−2 ln 0.05 + ln(1 − 2 ln 0.05)) / 100), fixed.
     - LB_t(α) = mean − σ̂_t · √( 2(tρ² + 1) / (t²ρ²) · ln( √(tρ² + 1) / α ) ), where σ̂_t is the sample standard deviation (n − 1) of the entered X_d.
     - LB_s is computed only for s ≥ 60. Fewer dates give an unstable σ̂, and an early spike would stick. The reported bound is the running maximum of LB_s(0.05) over 60 ≤ s ≤ t.
     - The anytime p-value is the smallest α for which the maximum over 60 ≤ s ≤ t of LB_s(α) is above 0.
     - For scale: ρ ≈ 0.2817. At t = 100 with σ̂ = 1, the bound sits about 0.30 below the mean. A one-off two-sided 95% interval would sit 0.196 below it, so this bound is about 1.55 times wider.
   - A confidence sequence stays valid however often it is checked, so the bound may be watched continuously.
6. **Claim rule.** "The pooled desk beats random" (a secondary result) may be stated only when all of these hold:
   - at least 300 event-days have resolved;
   - they span at least 60 cluster dates;
   - at least 4 lenses have each contributed at least 10 units;
   - the bound is above 0 after the Holm correction in item 8;
   - the statistic recomputed without the largest lens (the lens whose units have the largest total lift) is still above 0.
7. **Reported beside it, as exploratory:**
   - each lens's share of units;
   - the statistic without the largest lens;
   - the counts of watch-only and not-filled secrets.
8. **Multiple testing.**
   - H-POOL-A, H-POOL-B and every later pool version form one Holm family at α = 0.05, applied to the anytime p-values defined in item 5.
   - Later pool versions join this family for good.
   - The family is separate from the single-lens family of §7.4, which is unchanged.

## H-POOL-B: one combined clue in a shadow pair (planned; needs its own OK)

1. **When, and with what OK.**
   - Version 1 has no S-POOL accounts (§3 says no other accounts exist).
   - The shadow pair S-POOL-with and S-POOL-without starts only under a new version, `prereg/v2-pool.md`. That version needs a `decisions/` record quoting Spiro's yes to these two accounts, and it may start only once at least 4 lenses hunt with stamped tests.
   - `v2-pool` must use the parameters fixed below. Changing any of them makes it a different pool version, which still joins the Holm family in H-POOL-A item 8.
2. **Lens list.** Every hunting lens with a stamped test. Each lens's score definition is part of that lens's own stamped test, fixed before the lens contributes.
3. **Score.**
   - Each listed lens gives every open question it examines a score s in [−1, +1]: the direction and strength of its view on YES, from data available at that time only (§1).
   - A score of 0 is a real score. A lens with no view gives none.
4. **Combiner, fixed now:**
   - **Equal weights.** S = the mean of the scores present.
   - **Size of the nudge.** The fair-value nudge in log-odds is Δ = k · S, with **k = 0.2**. At 50¢ that moves fair value by at most about 5¢.
   - **Minimum coverage.** Questions with fewer than 2 scores get no nudge.
   - **No refitting.** A refitted combiner is a later pool version.
5. **Shadow pair.**
   - The pair runs in the same simulator, under the same fill rules, limits, universe and market base as A1.
   - It differs only by Δ.
   - It never changes A1–A7 or B1.
6. **Statistic and bound.**
   - Let D_d be (S-POOL-with profit − S-POOL-without profit) on scheduled close date d.
   - D_d covers only dates with a fill in either account. An event-day here is an (event, close date) pair with such a fill.
   - The bound is the H-POOL-A item 5 confidence sequence (same ρ, entry rule and formula) applied to D_d.
7. **Claim rule.** "The combined clue adds money" (a secondary result) may be stated only when all of these hold:
   - at least 300 event-days have resolved in the pair;
   - they span at least 60 dates;
   - the bound is above 0 after Holm;
   - §9's A5/A6 condition is not blocking P1 at that time. If the fill rules are too kind for P1, they are too kind for this too.

## For both hypotheses

- Only data received after each decision's time is used to score it (§1). No backtests of AI lenses.
- Results, including failures, appear on the private dashboard. They appear on the public page only under the venue gate (`config/data_permissions.yaml`).
- No extra AI spend: the pool reuses lens outputs.
- Any later change is a new version with its own addendum. This addendum is never edited.
