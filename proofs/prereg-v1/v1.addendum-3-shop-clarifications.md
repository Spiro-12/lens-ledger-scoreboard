# Pre-registration v1, addendum 3: shop clarifications (stamped before the first counted shop run)

**Status:** final (2026-11-17). Stamped with OpenTimestamps and published as a fingerprint before the first counted run of accounts A1–A7; never edited afterwards. It clarifies `prereg/v1.md` without changing any frozen parameter. It answers questions raised by the builder and by an independent review of the simulator (`docs/reviews/pr-15.md`). Where it chooses between readings, it takes the more conservative one, so that results are never kinder than reality.

**C1. Quote width.** Shaw & Dalen (arXiv 2510.15205), Eq. (9), as printed: `2δ ≈ γ·σ̄²·(T−t) + (2/k)·ln(1+γ/k)`. Classic Avellaneda–Stoikov writes `(2/γ)` in the second term. v1 follows the cited paper. Any other choice runs as a later version.

**C2. AI view.** Counted runs start with fair value = market base (§8: "Where no AI view exists, fair value = market base"). AI views from the `forecast` role are added once that pipeline is wired and reviewed. A1 then uses them where they exist; nothing else changes.

**C3. Price jump (§5 pulls).** For each taker side separately, compute the range of trade prices within 10 minutes. A jump is either side's range of more than 5¢ (at least 5.1¢ on the 0.1¢ grid), or a change of more than 5¢ in the market-base mid between consecutive decision books within 10 minutes, as §5 says. This keeps the bounce between bid and ask from counting as a jump.

**C4. Brackets with a one-sided book.** A bracket whose book lacks either side, whether one-sided or empty, uses its last trade price, of any age, if that price lies within whichever side is quoted. Otherwise A1 skips the whole event.

**C5. Same markets for the benchmarks.** A4, A5 and A6 quote only where A1 has a fair value (A1's quoting mask), so P1 compares like with like.

**C6. Post-only and adverse prints (§4.2, §4.4).**
- The rejection window starts at the receipt time of the decision book.
- A print crossing our price between go-live and the confirmation book rejects the quote.
- While a quote rests, a print on the opposite side at or through our price counts as an adverse fill for us at our price, up to its size after the line ahead. Such a print implies an order that would have traded with us.
- This adverse-fill rule applies to every account, including A2. A2's normal fills still need prints strictly through its price, but its adverse fills count at or through, so A2 is never kinder than A1.

**C7. Block and RFQ prints.** Prints flagged `is_block_trade` never fill us and never shorten the line ahead.

**C8. Gaps (§4.6).**
- "At or through" for a book means the best opposite price is at or through our price.
- No fill decision is made until a book received after the gap exists.
- A gap that hasn't been back-filled within 6 hours is treated as unrecoverable.

**C9. Universe draw (§2).**
- Trade counts allow a 10-minute ingest grace.
- Series categories come from the latest full series snapshot, of any age.
- Markets whose ticks cannot be represented in 0.1¢ units are ineligible.
- **First draw:** as soon as the trade tape has 24 hours of complete coverage and the reviewed simulator is merged. After that, every Monday 00:00 UTC, as written.
- Drawn tickers, plus every bracket ticker of the drawn events, are always kept by the collector's tape filter. Earlier markets still held stay on that keep list until finalized.
- **Which sweep.** A draw uses the first full sweep after Monday 00:00 UTC whose preceding 24 hours of tape are fully covered by coverage records received by sweep + 10 minutes. The first draw uses the first such sweep after the first logged `--draw-now` request that week. Full sweeps happen once a day, so it usually waits for the next day's sweep. It uses the same Monday seed.
- Trade counts use only trades received by that same cutoff, and block prints are not counted.
- A draw re-run at any later time gives the identical universe, and a saved draw is never replaced. Until a sweep qualifies there is no draw, and no counted quoting that week. A running shop stops at Monday 00:00 UTC and restarts only once the new week's draw is stamped.

**C10. Release calendar (§5 pulls).** Each week's calendar is the close times of all open markets in Kalshi's "Economics" category (they close at the data release), plus FOMC decision times from federalreserve.gov. It is saved and hashed with that week's universe. An Economics close pulls that series' markets; an FOMC decision pulls every Economics market.

**C11. Restarts.** Account state is rebuilt from the logs at startup: ledgers, fills, open orders, the daily-stop baseline and breakers. If the rebuild fails, the counted run does not start.

**C12. Off switch.** The HALT file is read-only (mode 0444) to the trading process and contains `ok` (or `run`) while trading is allowed; anything else stops it. The process refuses to run if the file is writable, and a test checks that no code path writes to it or changes its permissions. A separate operating-system user is required before any real money.

**C13. Interpretations judged conservative by the independent review, recorded here:**
- the clock-error band is applied to each comparison separately;
- for A2, "strictly through" means a print exactly at our price neither fills us nor shortens the line ahead;
- the taker-YES share is weighted by contracts;
- event and group limits add up each question's maximum loss;
- payouts on fractional contracts are rounded down to the cent;
- the losing-fill markout is measured from the mid at fill time;
- the line-ahead count uses only trades on our side.

**C14. Practice runs never count.** Builders ran short development tests on 2026-11-17 (UTC). Those tests wrote `decisions/*/shop_quotes-*` and `secrets/*/*` files into the private store before this addendum was stamped. They stay in the store and in the daily fingerprints unchanged, because nothing is ever deleted. They are never counted. A counted shop run starts only with a `shop_run_start` record that names this addendum's SHA-256 and the merged code commit. That commit is a clean copy of code merged into main. The run uses a frozen copy at that commit, so later changes can't alter it. Results use only records written by that run after that record. The same rule covers the Secrets Desk: a seal counts only once its own addendum is stamped.

**C15. Stale decisions.** A decision more than 60 seconds old by either clock (for example after the Mac slept) is dropped before it is logged, recorded as `stale_decision`, and made again from a fresh book.

**C16. Late cancels.** A pull cancel logged more than 60 seconds after its trigger takes effect at its write time plus 2 seconds, not at the trigger time. The order stayed live longer, so this can only add fills against us.

**C17. One record, every run.**
- The logged and stamped order and ledger records are the record. A restart only checks them; it never rewrites them.
- A restart that cannot prove the record (for example because a record is not covered by its timestamp) refuses. The run is then closed by the operator (`--close-run`) with a `shop_run_end` record, and the next counted start opens a new run.
- **P1–P3 and every report pool all counted runs**, from the first counted run's first decision. A closed run's results up to its last proven record stay in. Its open positions are valued at each market's finalized value, or at §9's cautious exit value, with no further fills. No run can be dropped.

**C18. Markouts.** For every fill, books are fetched at +1 minute, +10 minutes, +1 hour and +1 day. Held markets keep getting a book at least hourly until finalized while the shop is running. Markouts use the first book received at or after each horizon, and record how late it was, for example after a stop while the shop waits for the week's draw.

**C19. Replay uses what was seen.** When rebuilding, each step uses only the tape and books it had received when it ran, and the filtered set it logged. Data that arrived later never changes past steps.

**C20. Smaller rules that affect results.**
- A7 bids the remaining legs only while (held NO cost with fees) + (remaining legs at the best NO bid with maker fees) is under N − 1. A part-filled leg is bid only for what is missing.
- The 10% size cap uses Kalshi's `volume_24h_fp` (our own tape only if Kalshi's figure is missing).
- Markets whose trades the collector dropped are never quoted or simulated.
- A6 uses only the same run's A1 decisions.

Market base = the microprice: each side's best price weighted by the size showing on the opposite side.
